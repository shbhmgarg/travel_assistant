# Singapore: Sample Itineraries

## Three-Day Sightseeing Itinerary (Classic / First-Timer)

**Day 1 — Marina Bay & Civic District (mostly outdoor)**
Morning: Merlion Park and the Civic District's colonial architecture.
Afternoon: Marina Bay Sands and the ArtScience Museum (indoor option if
needed). Evening: Gardens by the Bay, including the Supertree Grove light
show.

**Day 2 — Culture & Heritage (mix of outdoor and indoor)**
Morning: Chinatown — temples and heritage shophouses. Afternoon: Little
India for spice markets and temples. Evening: Kampong Glam, Sultan Mosque,
and dinner around Haji Lane / Arab Street.

**Day 3 — Sentosa Island (mostly outdoor, indoor alternatives available)**
Full day on Sentosa: beaches, Universal Studios Singapore, or S.E.A.
Aquarium (a fully indoor option if weather turns).

## Three-Day Itinerary for Families with Children

**Day 1**: Singapore Zoo in the morning, River Wonders in the afternoon
(both largely outdoor but with covered/indoor exhibit areas).
**Day 2**: Gardens by the Bay's children's garden and the two indoor
conservatories (Flower Dome, Cloud Forest) — a good fully-indoor option
for a rainy day.
**Day 3**: Sentosa Island for beaches and Universal Studios Singapore.

## Indoor-Focused Itinerary (Rainy Day Plan)

For travellers wanting to minimise outdoor exposure (e.g. due to rain):
- ArtScience Museum (Marina Bay)
- Gardens by the Bay's Flower Dome and Cloud Forest conservatories
  (climate-controlled, fully indoor)
- National Museum of Singapore or the Asian Civilisations Museum
- Shopping malls along Orchard Road
- S.E.A. Aquarium on Sentosa

## Two-Day Express Itinerary

**Day 1**: Marina Bay Sands, Gardens by the Bay, Merlion Park.
**Day 2**: Chinatown in the morning, Little India or Kampong Glam in the
afternoon, Clarke Quay in the evening for dinner and nightlife.
