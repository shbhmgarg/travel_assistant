# How Visitors Pay for Public Transport in Singapore

## Singapore Tourist Pass (STP)

Visitors can purchase the Singapore Tourist Pass — a special EZ-Link
stored-value card offering unlimited travel for one, two, or three days.
Available at SimplyGo Ticket Offices located in selected MRT stations.

## Stored-Value Smartcards

Adult stored-value smartcards (EZ-Link or NETS FlashPay) cost $10, which
includes $5 of initial stored travel value. Available at:

- Train station Passenger Service Centres
- SimplyGo Ticket Offices
- Convenience stores such as 7-Eleven, Buzz, and Cheers

## Digital / Contactless Payment

Visitors can tap a foreign-issued Mastercard or Visa contactless bank card,
or a mobile wallet, directly at fare gates and on buses — no registration
needed. Note: administrative fees apply when paying with a foreign bank
card.

## Concession Fares for Children

- Children taller than 0.9m and under 7 years old can get a free Child
  Concession Card for basic bus and train travel.
- Children shorter than 0.9m travel free when accompanied by a paying
  passenger.

## Cash Payment (Buses Only)

Buses accept cash, but passengers must have exact change — drivers do not
give change or refunds.
