# Singapore Tourist Pass — Types and Pricing

The Singapore Tourist Pass (STP) is a special EZ-Link card that gives tourists
unlimited travel on Singapore's basic bus services, MRT (Mass Rapid Transit),
and LRT (Light Rail Transit) trains for a fixed number of days.

## 1. Singapore Tourist Pass (Standard)

Unlimited travel on basic bus, MRT, and LRT services.

| Duration | Price |
|----------|-------|
| 1 day    | $17   |
| 2 days   | $24   |
| 3 days   | $29   |
| 4 days   | $37   |
| 5 days   | $45   |

## 2. Singapore Tourist Pass Charm (Mr. Merlion – Round)

A collectible souvenir version of the pass, featuring the Merlion in a
playful, pocket-sized form. Offers the same unlimited basic transport access
as the standard pass.

| Duration | Price |
|----------|-------|
| 1 day    | $22   |
| 3 days   | $34   |

## 3. Singapore Tourist Pass + Changi Recommends Bundle

Combines the standard transit pass with extra travel bundle perks such as
travel insurance, guided tours, and travel concierge credits.

| Duration | Price |
|----------|-------|
| 1 day    | $19   |
| 2 days   | $28   |
| 3 days   | $48   |
| 4 days   | $52   |
| 5 days   | $60   |

## Usage Terms

- All passes cover unlimited travel on **basic** public transport only —
  premium services like the Sentosa Express and express buses are excluded.
- Multi-day passes must be used on **consecutive days**.
- Pass validity for a given day runs from first use until the end of
  service that day.

## Where to Buy

Passes can be bought via the official purchase pages, or in person at
EZ-Link vending machines located at Changi Airport terminals.
