# Singapore: Things to Do, by Category

## Outdoor Activities

- **Gardens by the Bay** (Supertree Grove and outdoor gardens)
- **Singapore Zoo and Night Safari**
- **Singapore Botanic Gardens** (UNESCO World Heritage Site, incl.
  National Orchid Garden)
- **Sentosa Island beaches**
- **Pulau Ubin** — cycling and nature exploration
- **Merlion Park** — waterfront photo spot
- **Southern Ridges** — elevated walking trail connecting several parks

## Indoor Activities (Good for Rainy Days)

- **ArtScience Museum** (Marina Bay)
- **Gardens by the Bay's Flower Dome and Cloud Forest** — these two
  conservatories are climate-controlled and fully indoor, despite being
  part of an otherwise outdoor park
- **National Museum of Singapore**
- **Asian Civilisations Museum**
- **S.E.A. Aquarium** (Sentosa)
- **Science Centre Singapore**
- **Shopping malls** along Orchard Road (ION Orchard, Paragon, etc.) —
  a practical fallback during heavy rain

## Family-Friendly Activities

- Singapore Zoo, River Wonders, and Night Safari
- Universal Studios Singapore (Sentosa)
- Science Centre Singapore
- Gardens by the Bay's children's garden

## Cultural Experiences

- Chinatown (temples, heritage shophouses, hawker food)
- Little India (temples, spice and textile markets)
- Kampong Glam (Sultan Mosque, Malay heritage)
- Civic District (colonial-era museums and architecture)

## Food and Local Experiences

- Hawker centres (Maxwell Food Centre, Lau Pa Sat) for inexpensive,
  high-quality local dishes
- Chinatown and Little India for concentrated cultural food experiences
- Clarke Quay for riverside dining and nightlife

## Shopping

- Orchard Road — Singapore's main shopping belt
- Haji Lane / Arab Street (Kampong Glam) — boutique and independent
  shopping
- Bugis Street — budget-friendly street shopping
