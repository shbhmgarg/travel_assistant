# Singapore: Essential Travel Information

## Climate

Singapore has a tropical climate that is warm and humid year-round, with
temperatures typically between 24-32°C (75-90°F). There isn't a strong
"dry" vs "wet" season distinction like in some tropical countries, but
short, heavy afternoon showers are common throughout the year, especially
during the Northeast Monsoon period (roughly November to January). Because
rain tends to arrive as brief, intense downpours rather than all-day rain,
many itineraries build in flexible indoor alternatives (museums,
conservatories, malls) for when a shower passes through.

## Language

English is an official language and the primary language of business and
government, so visitors can get by comfortably without speaking Mandarin,
Malay, or Tamil (the other three official languages). Signage is
predominantly in English.

## Currency and Payments

The local currency is the **Singapore Dollar (SGD)**. Contactless card
payment and mobile payment are widely accepted, including for public
transport fares. Cash is still useful at hawker centres and smaller
stalls, though this is changing.

## Connectivity

Singapore has extensive 4G/5G mobile coverage and free public Wi-Fi in
many areas (malls, MRT stations, some public spaces). Prepaid tourist SIM
cards are available at the airport for visitors who want a local number.

## Cultural and Practical Tips

- **Etiquette**: Singapore is famously clean and orderly; littering,
  jaywalking, and eating/drinking on public transport are fined offences.
- **Diversity**: Singapore's population reflects Chinese, Malay, Indian,
  and Eurasian heritage — visiting Chinatown, Little India, and Kampong
  Glam in a single day is a common way to experience this diversity.
- **Tipping** is not customary and not expected; many restaurants already
  add a service charge.
- **Dress**: Light, breathable clothing is recommended given the heat and
  humidity; modest dress is appreciated when visiting temples and
  mosques.
- **Safety**: Singapore is considered one of the safest cities in the
  world for travellers.

## Families with Children

Singapore is considered a strongly family-friendly destination. Sentosa
Island (beaches, Universal Studios Singapore), the Singapore Zoo and
River Wonders, Gardens by the Bay's children's garden, and the Science
Centre Singapore are commonly recommended for family itineraries. Many
malls also have dedicated family/nursing facilities.
